The Growth of Technology

1940s

Room-sized computers. The birth of electronic computation systems like ENIAC.

1980s

Personal Computers. Technology enters the home with microprocessors and GUI interfaces.

1990s

The Web Era. Internet and email become mainstream, connecting the world via the World Wide Web.

2000s

Mobile Mobility. Smartphones and social media redefine how we communicate on the go.

Today

The Intelligence Era. AI, Cloud Computing, and 5G networks create a truly hyper-connected world.
