What is ICT?

ICT stands for Information and Communication Technology. It is the use of computers, networks, and digital tools to create, store, process, and share information.

Learn More

Understanding ICT

ICT is all around us! Here are some simple examples of how we use it every day.

Creating

Using a computer to type documents for school or work.

Sharing

Sending quick messages to friends and family on WhatsApp.

Searching

Finding information about any topic using Google Search.

Communicating

Sending official letters or homework to teachers via Gmail.

ICT Fundamentals

The core building blocks that make technology work.

Networks

Two or more computers connected together to share resources and information.

  • The Internet
  • School Lab
  • Office Wi-Fi
  • Home Network

Software

Special programs and instructions that tell the computer hardware what to do.

  • MS Word
  • Google Chrome
  • Windows OS
  • Apps / Games

Data vs Information

Data (Raw)

Simple numbers or facts without meaning yet.

Example: 85, 90, 78
Information (Processed)

Data that has been processed to have meaning.

Example: "The student's average mark is 84."

Communication

Technology used to share information and stay connected with others.

  • Internet
  • Email
  • Video Calls
  • Telegram
  • Zoom

Storage Devices

Physical devices used to save your data and files for later use.

  • Hard Disk
  • USB Flash
  • Memory Card
  • CD / DVD

Hardware

Physical parts of a computer system that you can touch and feel.

  • Keyboard
  • Mouse
  • Monitor
  • Printer
  • Hard Disk

Generation

Computer Generations means the different stages of development of computers over time. Each generation shows improvements in technology, size, speed, and memory.

  • Generation 1 :- Vacuum Tubes
  • Generation 2 :- Transistors
  • Generation 3 :- Integrated Circuits
  • Generation 4 :- Large Scale Intergration
  • Generation 5 :- Very Large Scale Intergration
