Comparison Summary

Which one for me?

If you print school assignments every day, a Laser printer is cheapest over time. If you love printing high-quality family photos, Inkjet is your best choice.

The Future

3D Printing isn't just for toys. Scientists now use them to print prosthetic limbs and even small sections of buildings!
