2 Processing Unit

The CPU: Mastermind

The CPU (Central Processing Unit) handles calculations and unit coordination. It's built of three critical components:

ALU

Arithmetic Logic Unit

Performs all mathematical and logical comparisons.

CU

Control Unit

Directs data flow and manages system operations.

R

Registers

High-speed internal storage for immediate instructions.
